Smart Laundry

How It Works

The Smart Weather Laundry Scheduler converts real-time Lagos weather data into a single, clear answer: is today a good day for laundry?

The Laundry Decision Engine

1

Location Selection

You select your Lagos LGA (e.g., Ikeja, Lekki, Badagry). The system retrieves real-time forecast data specific to that location's coordinates from the Open-Meteo weather API.

2

Weather Processing

The engine analyses five weather variables: Temperature, Relative Humidity, Wind Speed, Rain Probability, and Active Precipitation — for both the current hour and the next 16 days.

3

Drying Score (0–100)

Each weather variable earns a weighted score contribution. High temperature and useful wind add points. High humidity and rain risk reduce points. If rain probability exceeds 70% or active precipitation exceeds 1mm, the score is hard-capped at 40.

Temperature · 20 pts Humidity · 25 pts Wind · 20 pts Rain · 30 pts Precipitation · 5 pts
4

Fabric & Load Personalisation

Each fabric type has a baseline drying factor (Silk: fastest, Towels: slowest). The washing method and load size are applied as time multipliers to estimate how long your specific load will take to dry under current conditions.

5

Best Drying Window Finder

The algorithm scans the hourly forecast to find a continuous block of hours that meets the estimated drying time — with low rain probability, preferably during daylight hours.

6

Actionable Recommendation

Based on the final score and rain risk, you receive a clear answer: Excellent (90+), Good (70–89), Moderate (50–69), or Poor (<50). You always know what to do next.
